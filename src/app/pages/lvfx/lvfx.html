Introduction

A screenplay contains an enormous amount of production information, but very little of it arrives in a form software can use directly.

For visual effects (VFX) teams, understanding what a project requires can involve reading the screenplay, identifying moments that may require visual effects, interpreting creative intent, anticipating shots, assets and effects, documenting assumptions and eventually translating that work into something that can be estimated financially.

To a reader, a screenplay appears reasonably structured. To software, it is much messier. Scene headings, dialogue, action, transitions, formatting conventions and writing styles vary between scripts.

The process of breaking down a screenplay into shots, assets, effects and assumptions is long and complex. It requires a deep understanding of the production process and the ability to make judgements about the intent of the screenplay. Constant revisions to the screenplay and evolving creative direction make the process that much more challenging.

Could AI help turn that early ambiguity into structured, useful production intelligence without pretending to replace the people making the decisions?

The challenge was not just building a system that could extract information from a screenplay, but also to build one that users could trust and use effectively.

In large movie productions, VFX budgets go well into the tens of millions of dollars. No producer would blindly trust a blackbox that ingests a screenplay and spits out a financial estimate. Every step of the process needs to be transparent and accountable.

In closing

Why this work matters to me

LVFX changed how I think about AI products.

The hard part usually isn't demonstrating that a model can do something impressive.

The hard part is understanding a profession well enough to decide what should be automated, what should become structured data, what needs to remain editable, where deterministic software is better, and where human judgement has to remain authoritative.

That is the kind of work I want to keep doing.
