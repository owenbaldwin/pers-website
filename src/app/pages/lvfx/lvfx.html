Introduction

A screenplay contains enormous amounts of production information, but almost none of it arrives in a structured form.

For VFX teams, understanding what a project requires can involve manually reading scripts, identifying relevant moments, interpreting creative intent, anticipating assets and effects, making assumptions and eventually translating all of that into something that can be estimated.

This isn't simply a document-extraction problem. Much of the valuable information is implicit, contextual and dependent on production judgement.

Could AI help turn that early ambiguity into structured, useful production intelligence without pretending to replace the people making the decisions?
