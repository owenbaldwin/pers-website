Introduction

Alongside my work building technology products, I work inside the facilities/IT team of a London music university, a vantage point I keep coming back to.

It's shown me that some of the highest-value automation opportunities are hidden inside mundane operational processes that nobody considers a technology problem.

Rather than starting with “Where can we use AI?”, I prefer to start with a different question.

Where are people repeatedly spending time on work that shouldn't require this much effort?

The problem: thousands of physical assets, checked by hand once a year.

Why this work matters to me

This wasn't a revolutionary piece of software. It was a small tool applied to the right operational bottleneck: five days of one person's time, working four hours a day, instead of multiple weeks with two.

Sometimes AI belongs inside the product. Sometimes AI simply lets you build the right product faster.

Knowing the difference is the interesting part.
