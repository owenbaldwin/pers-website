01

LVFX

AI-native VFX production & budgeting platform

Turning screenplays into structured production intelligence

Film and VFX planning begins with an unusually messy input: a screenplay.

I helped turn that unstructured starting point into a system capable of identifying production requirements, structuring scenes, shots, assets and effects, applying production assumptions, and connecting that information to downstream VFX planning and budgeting.

The difficult part wasn't simply calling an LLM. It was understanding what information production teams actually need, how those concepts relate to each other, what AI should decide, what humans should control, and how unreliable model output becomes a usable production workflow.

Positioning

A useful combination

I've had to move between conversations with domain experts, product decisions, data structures, AI behaviour, implementation and demos because building an early-stage product required all of them.

That's also the kind of work I want to continue doing.

I'm most useful where there is ambiguity at the beginning of the problem: a complex workflow, specialised users, emerging technology and no obvious specification yet.
