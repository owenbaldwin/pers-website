About

I build between disciplines.

My background isn't a conventional route into technology. I originally trained and worked in music and creative fields before becoming increasingly involved in software, AI and product development.

That combination eventually led me into film-production technology and LVFX, where I found myself working across product thinking, domain research, AI systems and implementation.

The common thread is less “engineering” than turning complicated ideas into systems people can actually use. I'm comfortable moving between a conversation about someone's workflow and a technical conversation about how we might represent or automate it.

I still build software and intend to remain technically capable. But I'm most interested in positions where technical ability is a means rather than the entire job: AI product, solutions, implementation, forward-deployed and technology strategy roles.

I'm particularly interested in AI applied to creative industries, media and specialised professional workflows, although I'm not limited to those sectors.
